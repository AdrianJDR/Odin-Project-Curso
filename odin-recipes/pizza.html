Pizza is a popular Italian dish made from a flat circle of bread dough. It is covered with tomato sauce, cheese (like mozzarella), and other foods like meat or vegetables, then baked very hot.

    Dough Base

  • Flour (all-purpose or bead flour)
  • Warm water
  • Yeast
  • Salt
  • Olive Oil
  • Sugar

    • Sauce and cheese

  • mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Tomato sause or crushed tomatoes

    • Common Toppings

  • Pepperoni or sliced sausage
  • Mushrooms and bell peppers
  • Fresh basil leaves

How to make the dish

    Prepare the Dougth

    • Mix the flour, yeast, salt, sugar, warm water, and olive oil in a bowl.
    • Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 10 minutes until smooth.
    • Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover it, and let it rise for 1 to 2 hours.

    Prep and Heat

    • Preheat your oven to its highest setting, usually between 450°F and 500°F (230°C to 260°C).
    • Punch down the risen dough to release air bubbles.
    • Roll or stretch the dough into your desired crust shape and thickness.

    Assemble and Bake

    • Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce evenly over the dough base.
    • Sprinkle a generous layer of mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
    • Add your favorite toppings like pepperoni, mushrooms, or vegetables.
    • Bake for 10 to 15 minutes until the crust is golden and cheese is bubbly.
